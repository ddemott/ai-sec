How to run it

  1. Click Start call session.
  2. Open spawned join link.
  3. Allow microphone.
  4. Use headphones unless you enjoy feedback loops. Browser gods do not protect the careless.
  5. Talk naturally. Interrupt. Change your mind. Make it sweat.

Good test prompts

  • Confused opener: “hello what is this”
  • Filler: “yeah uh-huh okay go on”
  • Meaningful interruption: “wait, I don’t get that part”
  • Mind change: “book repair for my laptop... actually it’s my desktop and it has malware”
  • Service in message: “tell Dale my wifi is down and computer has a virus”
