# SecretaryHQ — AI Receptionist for Service Businesses

---

## What It Is

SecretaryHQ is an AI-powered receptionist that answers your phones, books appointments, and handles customer inquiries around the clock — without hiring anyone.

You get a real phone number. When a customer calls, a natural-sounding AI voice picks up, has a real conversation with them, and books the appointment directly into your schedule. You never miss a call. You never lose a booking to voicemail. The AI sounds professional, knows your business inside and out, and works on days you are closed, in the middle of the night, and during every busy rush when you can not get to the phone.

It is not a phone tree. It is not a chatbot. It is a full conversation — your AI receptionist asks the right questions, checks real availability, and confirms the booking before the caller hangs up.

## The Problem It Solves

Every missed call is a missed customer. The average service business misses 30–40% of inbound calls — to voicemail, to hold, or simply to a busy owner who cannot step away from the work floor. Most of those callers do not leave a message. They call your competitor instead.

Hiring a receptionist solves the problem, but costs $35,000–$50,000 per year, only covers business hours, takes sick days, and still needs training. An answering service routes calls and takes messages, but does not book — the customer still has to wait for a callback.

SecretaryHQ answers instantly, books the appointment in real time, and never calls in sick.

## Core Features

### AI Phone Answering

Your business gets a dedicated phone number. When a customer calls, the AI picks up in seconds — no hold music, no voicemail, no "press 1 for appointments." The AI has a natural, human-sounding voice and carries a real back-and-forth conversation. It asks what the customer needs, checks your actual schedule, and confirms the booking before the call ends.

The AI knows your business: your services, your prices, your hours, your staff, your policies. If a customer asks "how long does a full synthetic oil change take?" or "do you do same-day appointments?" the AI answers correctly, from the information you set up during onboarding.

The AI voice is natural and configurable — not robotic, not a recording. Customers regularly finish the call without realizing they spoke to an AI.

### Appointment Booking

The AI checks real-time availability and books directly into your schedule. No double-booking. No gaps you did not intend. Bookings account for service duration, staff availability, and physical resources like bays, chairs, or equipment.

When a customer books with the AI, the appointment appears in your dashboard instantly. Your staff sees it. Your calendar sees it.

The AI handles:

- Checking staff and resource availability against your actual schedule
- Matching the right service to a qualified staff member
- Collecting service-specific information (vehicle make/model, tire size, preferred stylist, etc.)
- Confirming the appointment details before ending the call

### Customer Management (Built-in CRM)

Every caller becomes a customer record. SecretaryHQ tracks names, phone numbers, appointment history, notes, and service-specific details — automatically, from the first call. You can search, filter, and view any customer from the dashboard.

Custom fields adapt to your industry. An auto shop captures vehicle make, model, year, and mileage. A tire shop captures tire size and whether it is a roadside emergency. A salon records hair type, allergies, and stylist preference. The information is there every time that customer calls back.

### Knowledge Base and Policy Answering

Your AI receptionist knows the answers to the questions your customers ask most. Business hours, pricing, cancellation policies, parking, what to bring, what to expect — all of it comes from a knowledge base you build and own.

You can upload documents, write policies, or answer a structured questionnaire. The AI pulls from that knowledge to answer caller questions accurately, without putting anyone on hold to check.

### Your Dashboard

Everything your business needs in one place:

**Home** — Today's appointments, a week overview, quick booking for walk-ins and call-ins, and alerts for anything that needs attention.

**Schedule** — A full staff and resource scheduler showing who is working, what is booked, and where the open slots are. Day view shows all staff side by side, with color-coded appointments by service type. Calendar view shows the month at a glance.

**Customers** — Your full customer list with search, filtering, and detail profiles. View appointment history, add internal notes, and see everything the AI captured about each customer.

**Calls** — Every call your AI handled, with outcomes, transcripts, and AI-generated summaries. See who called, what they booked, and what was discussed — without listening to a single recording.

**Phone Assistant** — Configure your AI: its persona, voice, knowledge base, vocabulary, and how it handles edge cases. This is where you teach the AI about your business.

**My Business** — Your service catalog with durations, pricing, and skill requirements. Manage resources (bays, chairs, rooms). Set up integrations with your existing tools.

**My Team** — Employee profiles, working schedules, service assignments, and login access. Control who can do what, and when.

### Appointment Reminders

SecretaryHQ automatically sends SMS reminders to customers before their appointments. Fewer no-shows. No manual follow-up. Reminders go out on a schedule you control — one day before, two hours before, or both.

### Integrations

SecretaryHQ connects to the tools you already use:

**Calendars**

- Google Calendar — appointments sync both ways so your personal calendar stays current
- Microsoft Outlook — same bidirectional sync for Office 365 users

**CRM and Field Service**

- **Jobber** — popular with trades (plumbing, HVAC, landscaping); customer and job records stay in sync
- **HubSpot** — full contact and deal sync for businesses using HubSpot as their CRM
- **Square** — customer records sync with your Square account; works alongside Square's booking tools
- **ServiceTitan** — the industry standard for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical; customer and appointment data flows both directions

When a new customer calls and books, they appear in both SecretaryHQ and your connected CRM — no copy-paste, no duplicate entry.

### Multi-Staff and Multi-Resource Scheduling

SecretaryHQ handles real complexity. If you have five technicians, three bays, and six service types with different skill requirements, the AI navigates all of it. It will only book a service if the right staff member is on shift and the right resource is free. It will not book a tire mount if your only mount-certified technician is off that day.

Staff schedules are set by week and extended forward. You control working hours, days off, and which staff are qualified for which services. The AI works within those constraints automatically.

### Version History and Record Recovery

Every change to an appointment, customer record, or service is tracked. You can see what changed, when, and who changed it — and restore a previous version with one click. No data is permanently deleted without your confirmation.

### Role-Based Access

Not everyone on your team needs to see everything. SecretaryHQ has two staff roles:

- **Owner / Admin** — full access to all settings, staff, billing, integrations, and configuration
- **Front Desk** — access to the daily operational views (Home, Schedule, Customers, Calls) only

Owners can invite staff members by email and control what they can see.
