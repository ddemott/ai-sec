# DynaTire Mobile Services - Business Policies

## Operating Hours
Our mobile units operate from Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays to allow our team to rest. Emergency roadside assistance is available for existing commercial clients 24/7.

## Service Area
We serve the greater metropolitan area and surrounding suburbs within a 30-mile radius of the city center. A small travel fee of $25 applies to locations further than 20 miles from our main hub.

## Pricing & Payments
- Standard Tire Rotation: $60
- Flat Repair (On-site): $85
- Seasonal Tire Swap (on rims): $120
- Seasonal Tire Swap (off rims): $180
We accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept cash or personal checks.

## Cancellation Policy
We require at least 24 hours' notice for all cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice will incur a $50 "short-notice" fee. If our technician arrives at the location and the vehicle is not accessible, a $75 "no-show" fee will be applied.

## Warranty
All labor is guaranteed for 90 days. New tires purchased through DynaTire carry the full manufacturer's warranty.

## Customer Requirements
For on-site service, the vehicle must be parked on a flat, level surface (concrete or asphalt preferred). We cannot perform tire swaps on steep inclines or soft dirt/grass for safety reasons.
