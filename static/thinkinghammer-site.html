01The studio

Good software should work like a good tool — sharp where it counts, and smart enough to think for itself.

Thinking Hammer is an independent software studio that builds and ships its own products. No bloat, no roadmap theater — just well-engineered tools that solve real problems for real businesses.

The name is the brief. A hammer does serious work; the thinking part is what we add on top — software that doesn't just sit there waiting for input, but actually carries its share of the load.
